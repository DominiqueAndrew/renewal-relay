## Permanent evidence gate

Before presenting or shipping any material product, architecture, safety, or
judge-facing claim:

1. identify the claim and its uncertainty;
2. support it with a primary official source, reproducible experiment, or local
   implementation/test evidence;
3. record the source, assumptions, equations/metrics where relevant, and limits in
   [SCIENCE_APPENDIX.md](SCIENCE_APPENDIX.md);
4. distinguish observed, calculated, estimated, planned, and unverified states.

Do not use a blog as proof when an official document or primary paper exists. Do not
claim live Gemini, Cloud Run, Firestore, external side effects, or Devpost submission
without live evidence.
